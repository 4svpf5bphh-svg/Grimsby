Features & Feats
Tap-free quick reference for now
Wild Shape
Bonus Action transformation. Grimsby keeps his own HP. Moon forms gain 3 × Druid level Temp HP and use the Moon Druid AC rule.
Wild Resurgence
When Wild Shape is empty, spend a spell slot to regain one use. Once per Long Rest, spend one Wild Shape use to regain a level 1 spell slot. Interactive controls are in Combat → Resources.
Primal Order: Magician
Extra Druid cantrip and Wisdom-based bonus to the selected Intelligence skill check.
Familiar Friend
Find Familiar is always prepared with one free cast per Long Rest; Raven HP and Helpful Friend uses scale automatically.
Conjuration Adept
Wisdom increase already included. Conjuration spell access and concentration benefit are tracked by the rules engine.
Circle of the Moon
Improved Wild Shape forms, Moon-specific transformed defenses, and subclass spellcasting while transformed where allowed.
Orc: Adrenaline Rush
Bonus Action Dash; Temp HP and uses scale with proficiency bonus.
Orc: Relentless Endurance
When reduced to 0 HP without being killed outright, drop to 1 HP instead. Once per Long Rest. Tracked in Combat.
Orc: Darkvision
Darkvision 120 ft.
